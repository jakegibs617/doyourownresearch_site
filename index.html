04 / Why this exists

Curiosity deserves better infrastructure.

Do Your Own Research is an open-method research engine built to resist the easiest failure mode in knowledge work: collecting only the information that supports the answer you already like.

It frames the question, proposes competing explanations, gathers traceable evidence, and attacks its own leading theory before publishing. This site is the public record of that work.
