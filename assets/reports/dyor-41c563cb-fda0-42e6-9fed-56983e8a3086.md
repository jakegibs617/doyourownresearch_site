## 1. Research Question

To what extent did Project MKUltra's documented human subjects research activities involve non-consensual experimentation, and what were the program's stated objectives, methods, and institutional oversight failures?

## 2. Scope

- **Population:** Human subjects (witting and unwitting) involved in CIA-sponsored or CIA-funded behavioral, psychological, and pharmacological research; researchers, contractors, and institutions receiving CIA funding under this and related subprojects
- **Geography:** Primarily United States, with some subprojects and activities extending to Canada and other allied countries where CIA-funded research was conducted
- **Period:** 1953 to 1973 (official program duration as documented), with relevant precursor programs (e.g., Project BLUEBIRD, Project ARTICHOKE) from 1950 and aftermath including 1975-1977 Congressional investigations and document releases

### Out of scope

- Detailed technical/scientific evaluation of the validity of research findings produced under MKUltra
- Broader Cold War intelligence history not directly tied to MKUltra's human experimentation activities
- Post-1977 CIA behavioral research programs not derived from or continuous with MKUltra
- Civil litigation outcomes and individual compensation claims by test subjects, except as they bear on documenting scope of activities
- Popular culture depictions and fictionalized treatments of MKUltra

### Ambiguities the question recorded

- The full scope of MKUltra remains uncertain because most records were ordered destroyed in 1973; conclusions may differ depending on whether the question is scoped to documented/surviving records only or presumed full extent
- Whether 'MKUltra' should be treated as coextensive with the broader family of related CIA programs (e.g., MKDELTA, MKNAOMI, Project ARTICHOKE) or as a narrower, distinct program affects population and period boundaries
- The number of subprojects and total human subjects affected varies across sources (Church Committee, Rockefeller Commission, subsequent FOIA releases), which would change quantitative answers

### Assumptions the question recorded

- This scope treats 'documented' evidence as that surfacing from the 1977 Senate Church Committee hearings, the 1975 Rockefeley Commission, and the roughly 20,000 surviving financial documents located after the 1973 destruction order, rather than speculative or unverified claims
- Related precursor and successor programs (BLUEBIRD, ARTICHOKE) are treated as contextual background rather than within the core population unless explicitly designated as MKUltra subprojects

## 3. Definitions

- MKUltra: the CIA's classified program of human experimentation, officially initiated in 1953, involving behavioral modification, interrogation techniques, and drug testing (notably LSD), organized into numbered subprojects
- Non-consensual experimentation: research conducted without informed consent, including covert drug administration and psychological manipulation
- Institutional oversight failure: absence or circumvention of ethical review, legal authorization, or accountability mechanisms governing the research
- Subproject: a discrete, separately funded research activity conducted under the MKUltra umbrella, often at universities, hospitals, prisons, or through front organizations
