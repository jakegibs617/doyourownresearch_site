## 1. Research Question

To what extent, and among which populations, has belief in a flat Earth (as opposed to a round/globe Earth) persisted or grown in the modern era, and what factors explain its persistence?

## 2. Scope

- **Population:** Adults and adolescents in the general public who express or hold views on the shape of the Earth, including self-identified 'Flat Earthers' and members of organized flat-earth movements/communities
- **Geography:** Not geographically bounded by default; where data exists, comparisons across countries (e.g., United States, United Kingdom, Brazil) are of interest, since survey evidence on this topic is geographically uneven
- **Period:** Approximately 2015 to present, reflecting the resurgence of organized flat-earth advocacy associated with social media and platforms like YouTube, though historical antecedents (e.g., 19th-century flat-earth societies) may be referenced for context

### Out of scope

- The scientific validity or factual truth of flat-earth versus globe-earth claims
- Broader conspiracy theory belief in general, except where directly relevant to explaining flat-earth belief specifically
- Historical cosmological models predating the 19th-century flat-earth revival, except as brief context
- Astronomical or geodetic evidence for Earth's shape
- Legal, educational policy, or platform-moderation responses to flat-earth content
