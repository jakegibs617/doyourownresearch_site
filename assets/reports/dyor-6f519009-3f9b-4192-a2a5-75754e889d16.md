## 1. Research Question

What does the available evidence indicate about the validity of claims that the September 11, 2001 attacks were orchestrated or knowingly allowed by elements of the United States government, as opposed to being carried out solely by al-Qaeda as an external terrorist attack?

## 2. Scope

- **Population:** Public claims, investigative findings, and evidentiary materials (physical, forensic, testimonial, documentary) concerning the planning, execution, and prior knowledge of the September 11, 2001 attacks, including official investigations, independent research, and 'Truth movement' analyses.
- **Geography:** United States (site of the attacks: World Trade Center in New York City, the Pentagon in Arlington, Virginia, and Shanksville, Pennsylvania), with attention to international actors (e.g., al-Qaeda, Afghanistan) implicated in planning.
- **Period:** Primarily September 11, 2001 itself, with relevant scope extending from the mid-1990s (origins of the al-Qaeda plot and prior intelligence) through the present day (ongoing investigations, declassifications, and 'Truther' claims post-2001).

### Out of scope

- General critiques of U.S. foreign policy or intelligence failures prior to 9/11 that do not allege deliberate orchestration or complicity
- Analysis of the wars in Afghanistan and Iraq that followed 9/11, except where directly relevant to motive claims within inside-job theories
- Assessment of unrelated conspiracy theories about other events (e.g., other terrorist attacks) invoked only for comparative rhetorical purposes
- Legal or civil liability questions concerning victims' compensation or lawsuits unrelated to the causal question of who orchestrated the attacks
- Psychological or sociological analysis of why conspiracy theories about 9/11 persist or spread, except insofar as it bears on characterizing the claims themselves

### Ambiguities the question recorded

- Whether 'inside job' as used in the input refers to active government orchestration, or the weaker claim of foreknowledge combined with deliberate inaction ('Let It Happen On Purpose'), since these have different evidentiary bars and are often conflated in public discourse.
- Whether the question is asking for an evaluation of the truth of these claims, or a mapping of what claims exist and what evidence has been offered for and against them without adjudicating validity.
- Whether 'government' in this context means the executive administration in office in 2001, specific agencies (CIA, NSA, FBI), or a broader unspecified set of institutional actors, as different inside-job theories implicate different specific parties.

### Assumptions the question recorded

- The question is scoped as an evaluation of evidence and claims rather than a request to affirm or promote a particular conclusion, consistent with the instruction not to assert that anything is true.
- The relevant comparison baseline is the official U.S. government account (9/11 Commission Report and associated technical investigations) attributing the attacks to al-Qaeda operatives acting without U.S. governmental foreknowledge or complicity.

## 3. Definitions

- \'Inside job' refers to the hypothesis that agents or officials of the U.S. government (or affiliated actors) had foreknowledge of, participated in planning, deliberately allowed, or actively executed the September 11 attacks, as distinct from the attacks being solely conceived and carried out by al-Qaeda operatives.
- \'Evidence' includes physical/forensic analysis (e.g., structural engineering reports on building collapses), official investigative findings (e.g., the 9/11 Commission Report, NIST reports), declassified government documents, and claims made by researchers, journalists, or advocacy groups.
- \'Official account' refers to the findings of the 9/11 Commission and associated U.S. government investigative bodies (FBI, NIST, CIA) attributing sole responsibility to al-Qaeda.
- \'Truth movement' or 'Truther' claims refer to the body of alternative theories proposing government complicity, controlled demolition, or foreknowledge without warning.
