## 1. Research Question

What is the historical origin and content of conspiracy theories alleging that the Illuminati (or a successor secret group) is orchestrating a 'New World Order' to establish global totalitarian control, and how have these theories spread and evolved over time?

## 2. Scope

- **Population:** Proponents, promoters, and public audiences of Illuminati/New World Order conspiracy theories; historical secret societies and individuals referenced in these narratives (e.g., the original Bavarian Illuminati, later claimed successors)
- **Geography:** Global, with primary focus on Europe (origin) and the United States (major site of modern propagation)
- **Period:** Late 18th century (founding of the Bavarian Illuminati, 1776) through the present day

### Out of scope

- Adjudicating whether the Illuminati or a New World Order actually exists or currently operates as described in the theories
- Detailed doctrinal history of the Bavarian Illuminati's internal Enlightenment-era philosophy independent of its conspiratorial afterlife
- Other unrelated conspiracy theories not connected to the Illuminati/New World Order narrative (e.g., unrelated secret society theories like the Freemasons, except where directly intersecting)
- Legal or law-enforcement investigations into specific groups accused of being 'the Illuminati'
- Psychological or sociological theories of why individuals believe in conspiracy theories generally, except as directly relevant to this theory's spread

### Ambiguities the question recorded

- Whether 'New World Order' should be scoped only to its conspiracy-theory sense or also include mainstream political usages of the term that sometimes get conflated with it
- Whether the question should include only English-language/Western sources or also non-Western manifestations of similar theories
- Whether 'successor group' claims (e.g., Bilderberg Group, Council on Foreign Relations, Freemasons) should be treated as within scope when they are explicitly invoked as continuations of the Illuminati narrative

### Assumptions the question recorded

- Assumes 'New World Order' in this context refers to the conspiratorial usage rather than the mainstream geopolitical phrase
- Assumes secondary conspiracy theories (e.g., Bilderberg, CFR, Freemasons) are in scope only insofar as they are explicitly framed by proponents as continuations of the Illuminati/NWO narrative, not as independent subjects

## 3. Definitions

- \'Illuminati' refers both to the historical Bavarian Illuminati (1776-1785) and to the modern conspiratorial concept of a secretive elite group allegedly descended from or inspired by it
- \'New World Order' refers to the conspiracy theory concept of a planned totalitarian one-world government, distinct from the non-conspiratorial diplomatic/political usage of the phrase (e.g., post-Cold War international order rhetoric)
- \'Conspiracy theory' refers to an explanatory narrative attributing significant events or global control to a secret, malevolent group, without asserting the truth or falsity of such claims
- \'Spread and evolution' includes literature, media, political rhetoric, and internet/social media propagation
