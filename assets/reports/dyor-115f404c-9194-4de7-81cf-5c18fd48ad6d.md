## 1. Research Question

What evidence exists regarding claims that the World Health Organization has used vaccination programs to covertly sterilize populations?

## 2. Scope

- **Population:** Recipients of WHO-associated or WHO-recommended vaccination programs, particularly in contexts where sterilization allegations have been publicly raised (e.g., tetanus toxoid campaigns, polio campaigns)
- **Geography:** Global, with specific attention to countries where sterilization allegations have arisen (e.g., Kenya, Philippines, Mexico, Nicaragua, India)
- **Period:** 1990s to present, covering the period since tetanus-toxoid sterilization allegations first emerged through current vaccination programs

### Out of scope

- General critiques or safety concerns about vaccines unrelated to fertility or sterilization
- Voluntary, consented population control or family-planning programs explicitly labeled as such
- Non-WHO actors' family planning or population policies not linked to WHO vaccination campaigns
- Broader conspiracy theories about vaccine ingredients (e.g., microchips) not related to sterility claims
- Legal or political consequences of sterilization allegations (e.g., vaccine boycotts) except as evidence of the claim's spread

### Ambiguities the question recorded

- Whether 'the world' implies a single unified global conspiracy or a set of separate, unrelated national-level allegations
- Whether 'trying to' implies an ongoing intent-based claim requiring evidence of institutional intent, or simply an effect-based claim about outcomes of vaccination campaigns
- Whether the question is asking for an assessment of the truth of the sterilization claim, or a survey of the claim's origins, evidence basis, and how it has been investigated/rebutted
- Whether specific vaccines (tetanus toxoid, polio) are the intended focus or whether the claim covers all WHO-linked vaccination programs generically

### Assumptions the question recorded

- The question is treated as a request to survey and assess the evidentiary basis for sterilization allegations against WHO vaccination campaigns, not to presuppose the claim's truth or falsity
- \'WHO trying to sterilize the world' is interpreted as referring to specific, documented allegations of covert sterilization via vaccines rather than a literal claim of total global sterilization
- Analysis will draw on cases where these allegations have been formally investigated (e.g., by health ministries, independent laboratories, or fact-checking organizations)

## 3. Definitions

- \'Sterilize' refers to rendering an individual or population permanently or temporarily infertile without informed consent, as distinct from voluntary contraceptive or family-planning interventions
- \'The world' is treated as a rhetorical claim of a global or population-wide covert program rather than a literal universal target
- \'WHO' refers to the World Health Organization as an institutional actor, distinct from national health ministries or other organizations (e.g., Gates Foundation) that sometimes co-fund WHO-linked campaigns
- Vaccination refers to formally administered immunization campaigns (e.g., tetanus toxoid, oral polio) that have been the subject of sterilization claims
