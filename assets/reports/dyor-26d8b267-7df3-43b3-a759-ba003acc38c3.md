## 1. Research Question

What is the origin, content, and evidentiary basis of claims that the 1969-1972 Apollo Moon landings were staged or fabricated, and how do these claims hold up against the physical, photographic, and independent third-party evidence available?

## 2. Scope

- **Population:** Public claims, arguments, and evidence put forward by proponents and debunkers of the Moon landing hoax theory, including primary Apollo mission records and independent verification sources (e.g., non-US tracking, retroreflector experiments, lunar sample analyses)
- **Geography:** Primarily United States (as the claim concerns NASA's Apollo program), with attention to independent corroborating or contradicting evidence from other nations' space and scientific programs (e.g., Soviet Union tracking data, other countries' lunar laser-ranging observatories)
- **Period:** 1969 to 1972 for the Apollo crewed lunar landing missions themselves; 1970s to present for the emergence, evolution, and persistence of hoax claims and rebuttals

### Out of scope

- Unmanned lunar missions and probes not related to the crewed Apollo program
- General conspiracy theory psychology or sociology beyond this specific case, except where directly relevant to explaining the claim's persistence
- Other space program controversies unrelated to the Moon landing (e.g., Mars mission conspiracy claims)
- Detailed technical engineering specifications of Apollo spacecraft beyond what is needed to address specific hoax claims

### Ambiguities the question recorded

- Whether 'the Moon Landing Hoax' as a topic should be scoped as an inquiry into the hoax theory's claims and their validity, or as a sociological/historical inquiry into who believes it and why, since these are different research questions with different evidence bases
- Whether the scope should include only Apollo 11 or all six crewed landing missions (Apollo 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 17)

### Assumptions the question recorded

- The question is scoped as an evidentiary inquiry into the claims themselves and their evidentiary support, rather than a sociological study of hoax belief prevalence or psychology, since no specific angle was indicated in the input
- All six crewed Apollo landing missions (1969-1972) are included within scope, not just Apollo 11, since hoax claims commonly reference the broader program

## 3. Definitions

- \'Moon landing hoax' refers to the claim that NASA's Apollo 11 and subsequent crewed lunar landings did not actually occur on the Moon and were instead fabricated, staged, or simulated
- \'Evidentiary basis' refers to specific pieces of evidence cited by hoax proponents (e.g., photographic anomalies, flag movement, radiation belts) and the corresponding scientific or documentary responses
- \'Independent verification' refers to confirmation of Apollo landings via sources not controlled by NASA, such as foreign tracking stations, retroreflector arrays, or third-party lunar imaging
